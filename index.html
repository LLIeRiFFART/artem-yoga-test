Crescent Lunge

Anjaneyasana

Step your right foot forward, lower the back knee and sweep the arms overhead. Lift the chest and let the front of the hip open.

Inhale · reach up
02 / 05

Extended Side Angle

Utthita Parsvakonasana

Open the hips to the side, forearm to thigh and top arm long over the ear. One clean line from back heel to fingertips.

Exhale · lengthen
03 / 05

Triangle

Trikonasana

Straighten the front leg, reach forward and tip down. Stack the shoulders and gaze softly at the raised hand.

Inhale · expand
04 / 05

Standing Side Bend

Parsva Tadasana

Come up to standing, interlace the fingers overhead and bend like a palm in the wind. Feel the ribs breathe on one side.

Exhale · soften
05 / 05

Goddess Fold

Utkata Konasana

Wide stance, toes out, sink the hips and fold forward with fingertips to the floor. Let the head be heavy and the jaw unclench.
